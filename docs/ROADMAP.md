# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Verified organisation pilot

One bounded Galle region; verified bakeries/canteens/restaurants; selected community organisations; agreed collection points; trained coordinators; professionally reviewed handling protocols; monitored incident response.

## Phase 2 — Controlled household pilot

Invite verified households gradually, add onboarding/training, photo/label review, stronger traceability, explicit operating windows and participant feedback loops.

## Phase 3 — Volunteer logistics and notifications

Availability schedules, capacity/vehicle needs, route planning, delivery proof, escalation, and consented multilingual SMS/push through locally appropriate vendors.

## Phase 4 — Professional safety partnerships

Versioned rules, temperature records, audit tooling, recalls, allergen controls, training certification and formal partnerships with food-safety professionals and relevant authorities.

## Phase 5 — Authority and waste routing

Subject to legal/policy approval, integrate local authorities and vetted composting, organic-waste and animal-feed review partners for food that cannot go to people.

Every phase has a stop/go review covering recipient experience, safety incidents, privacy, partner workload, equity and operational reliability. Nationwide household rollout is not the initial recommendation.
