# Goals and non-goals

## Goals

- Demonstrate household and organisation food-rescue workflows end to end.
- Demonstrate safety screening with auditable green, amber and red decisions.
- Demonstrate English, Sinhala and Tamil interface switching.
- Preserve recipient dignity through aliases, approximate locations and collection points.
- Demonstrate volunteer logistics, incidents, role oversight and activity-derived impact.
- Provide a polished, mobile-first, repeatable presentation prototype.

## Non-goals

- Production food-safety certification or a guarantee of safe food.
- Government or Public Health Inspector approval.
- Payments, real identity verification, live SMS/email/push, or live logistics optimisation.
- Medical or legal advice.
- Production-scale security, availability, observability or infrastructure.
- Scientific environmental-impact calculations.
